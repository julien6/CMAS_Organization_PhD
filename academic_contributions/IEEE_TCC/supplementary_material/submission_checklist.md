# ScholarOne Submission Checklist

## Manuscript

- Main manuscript PDF compiled from `paper.tex`.
- Exactly 12 pages for Regular Paper submission.
- References included in the page count.
- Preliminary CLOUD 2025 version cited and disclosed.
- No unresolved citations or references.

## Cover Letter

- Upload `cover_letter_TCC.pdf` or the generated PDF from `cover_letter_TCC.tex`.
- Ensure the letter states traditional publication mode.
- Ensure the letter lists the substantial differences from IEEE CLOUD 2025.

## Supplementary Material

- Upload `KARMA_TCC_supplementary_material.zip` as a separate supplementary file.
- Do not merge supplementary material into the main manuscript PDF.

## ScholarOne Metadata

- Manuscript type: Regular Paper.
- Publication model: Traditional.
- Prior publication / extended version: disclose IEEE CLOUD 2025 paper.
- Confirm all authors and affiliations.
- Confirm acknowledgments and funding text.
