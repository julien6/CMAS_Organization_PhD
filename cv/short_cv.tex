\section{Professional Profile}

Doctor in Computer Science specializing in Multi-Agent Systems, Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL), and neuro-symbolic AI for cyberdefense and operational decision support.
I bridge research-grade AI methods with deployable architectures through R\&D experience with \textbf{Thales Land \& Air Systems}, \textbf{Thales Cyber Solutions}, the \textbf{University of Luxembourg}, and public-sector partners.
My work emphasizes robust autonomous systems, human-in-the-loop supervision, explainability, simulation, and operational constraints in safety-critical environments.

\section{Technical and R\&D Interests}

MARL; Multi-Agent System Organizations; Neuro-Symbolic AI; Cyberdefense \& Intelligent Autonomous Agents; Simulation, World Models \& Digital Twins; Safety-critical and Adversarial Environments; Software Architecture for MAS; Explainability and Human-in-the-loop AI.

\section{Research \& Industrial Projects}

\cvitem{\textcolor{blue}{ALIAS}}{\qquad Applied R\&D project within \textbf{Thales Cyber Solutions} for External Attack Surface Management (EASM). The platform combines reinforcement learning, MARL, world models, large language models, and neuro-symbolic AI in a human-in-the-loop framework for reliable, explainable, and adaptive cyber defense.}

\bigskip

\cvitem{\textcolor{blue}{AssistDiFo}}{\qquad Applied R\&D project with the Luxembourg Police to assist investigators in analyzing large volumes of heterogeneous digital evidence. The platform uses named entity recognition, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and large language models while keeping investigators in control of the workflow.}

\bigskip

\cvitem{\textcolor{blue}{\href{https://github.com/julien6/CybMASDE}{CybMASDE}}}{\qquad A research platform combining MARL, organizational modeling, and cyberdefense simulation to support the design and analysis of intelligent multi-agent architectures (with application for \textbf{Thales}-related projects).}

\bigskip

\cvitem{\textcolor{blue}{\href{https://github.com/julien6/MOISE-MARL}{MOISE+MARL}}}{\qquad A proof-of-concept framework integrating organizational roles, missions, and goals into MARL environments to guide learning and improve agent coordination.}
